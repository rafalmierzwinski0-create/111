03 Why trust it?

It is built to never break your page.

  • Google down? The last good copy stays. Visitors never see an error or an empty table.
  • Served from your server. Fetched in the background, so nobody waits.
  • A real <table>. Readable by search engines, even without JavaScript.
  • Private by design. No analytics, no account with us — it only talks to Google.
